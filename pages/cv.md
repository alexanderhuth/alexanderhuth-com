## Omio

*Head of SEO, 2013–2015 · Head of SEO & Content Marketing, 2015–2017 ·
Director Organic Acquisition, 2017–2019*

Omio brought me in to build their organic function, on the strength of what
I’d learned standing up teams at Rocket Internet. It ran in three phases
over six years.

**Build.** Established SEO end to end — strategy, technical foundations,
content production, and the team to run it — growing to 30 people across
10 markets in the first two years.

**Scale.** Doubled the team to 60 and coverage to 30 markets in 20
languages, adding content marketing to the remit and building a
localisation model that held quality consistent across all of them. We
built in-house rather than buying from agencies, hiring juniors and
developing them into ICs and managers.

**Broaden.** Widened the function beyond search to cover content marketing,
editorial, social, and community, running a matrix that could deliver by
market or by function depending on the quarter’s priorities.

The company went from 10 to 300 people over the same period.
