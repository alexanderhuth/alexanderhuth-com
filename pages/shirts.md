## Notes

<div class="shirt-note" id="alvvays" markdown="1">
**Alvvays** — Came bundled with the Alvvays' **Blue Rev** vinyl, released in 2022.
</div>

<div class="shirt-note" id="arcade-fire" markdown="1">
**Arcade Fire** — I bought this t-shirt at my first Arcade Fire show in 2010, on The Suburbs tour. Showing that giant billboard from behind is such a cool design, especially as it makes reading the band name harder.
</div>

<div class="shirt-note" id="bodi-bill" markdown="1">
**Bodi Bill** — Next Time, [Be Home Before Dinner](https://www.tapelink.io/share/G66V8rFU).
</div>

<div class="shirt-note" id="bon-iver" markdown="1">
**Bon Iver** — Most likely bought at the 2012 Bon Iver show at Arena. T-shirt designed by Bradley Hale of Now, Now.
</div>

<div class="shirt-note" id="broken-social-scene" markdown="1">
**Broken Social Scene** — Got this during the "20 Years of City Slang" weekend.
</div>

<div class="shirt-note" id="charizma" markdown="1">
**Charizma & Peanut Butter Wolf** — Their vinyl weighs a ton, their shirt weighs a ton. Heavy fabric, non-breathable print...
</div>

<div class="shirt-note" id="death-from-above-1979" markdown="1">
**Death From Above 1979** — Band shirt with a print of a photo of a guy wearing a band shirt with the logo of a band called Death From Above 1979. Bought at their 2015 Postbahnhof show.
</div>

<div class="shirt-note" id="dendemann" markdown="1">
**Dendemann** — Way too big, way too orange, and way too offensive to some people. Still, always loved this cartoon version of one of Dendemann's smartest lines:

> Vielleicht hab ich 'n Stock im Arsch und führ' 'n Spießerleben,  
> doch mein Zeige- und dein Mittelfinger würden "Peace" ergeben. 
> 
> *[Eins Zwo – Ey Du (Zwei, 2001)](https://tapelink.io/share/vf1J2CcU)*

Bought this shirt at my first Dendemann show, back in 2007.
</div>

<div class="shirt-note" id="grizzly-bear" markdown="1">
**Grizzly Bear** — Bought at the 2012 Berlin show at Astra. The band shirt was later featured on the poster of the 2014 film [The Skeleton Twins](https://boxd.it/7bCS).
</div>

<div class="shirt-note" id="hundreds" markdown="1">
**Hundreds** — Bought at one of their earliest shows.
</div>

<div class="shirt-note" id="hype-machine" markdown="1">
**Hype Machine** — While I don't obsess over finding new tracks anymore, I'm glad that [Hype Machine](https://hypem.com/popular) is still alive and kicking.
</div>

<div class="shirt-note" id="kilians" markdown="1">
**Kilians** — Bought at their 2013 farewell show in Berlin. Quality-wise, probably the worst band shirt I've ever owned.
</div>

<div class="shirt-note" id="la-dispute" markdown="1">
**La Dispute** — Should have ironed this one, hm? Bought on La Dispute's 2011-2012 Wildlife tour. [The shirt design features a house/home, guess the album the album is pretty emo...](https://record.club/alexanderh/lists/you-cant-spell-home-without-emo)
</div>

<div class="shirt-note" id="menomena" markdown="1">
**Menomena** — Design by member of the band, Danny Seim. Made for the tour promoting their 2010 album, Mines. I bought the shirt at their final Berlin show in 2012.
</div>

<div class="shirt-note" id="radiohead" markdown="1">
**Radiohead** — Bootleg concert shirt (allegedly made from recycled plastic bottles) bought outside the venue after their 2012 concert at Wuhlheide.
</div>

<div class="shirt-note" id="sinnbus" markdown="1">
**Sinnbus** — Sinnbus ❤️
</div>

<div class="shirt-note" id="the-appleseed-cast" markdown="1">
**The Appleseed Cast** — I think I bought this one in 2013, at their Privatclub show. The sleeves of the shirt were so wide, *Oma Else* had to make them tighter for the shirt to look even remotely okay on me.
</div>

<div class="shirt-note" id="the-kooks" markdown="1">
**The Kooks** — Bought at my first Berlin gig in December 2006. We drove to the big city on a school night!
</div>

<div class="shirt-note" id="the-postal-service" markdown="1">
**The Postal Service** — The "Bow & Arrow" tee was the first of 3 The Postal Service shirts I have owned over the years. The other one pictured I bought during The Postal Service's **Give Up 10th Anniversary Tour**. Traveled to Paris just for this show and came back with a shirt that has a side A (2003) and a side B (2013).
</div>

<div class="shirt-note" id="the-robocop-kraus" markdown="1">
**The Robocop Kraus** — Who do they think they are? The Robocop Kraus!
</div>

<div class="shirt-note" id="those-dancing-days" markdown="1">
**Those Dancing Days** — One of my favorite band shirt designs.
</div>

<div class="shirt-note" id="why" markdown="1">
**WHY?** — Never really loved WHY?'s **Mumps, Etc.** album, but the concert shirt featuring the whale (Monstro, is it you?) from the album cover was amazing. Especially the fin on the back.
</div>
